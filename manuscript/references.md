## Notes on two entries

**[3] and [4]** are GMD technical reports and carry no DOI, which is normal for
that series. Report 148 is consistently dated 2001. Report 152 appears in the
literature as both 2001 and 2002; we use 2002, and the year should be checked
against the report itself before submission. Report 148 also circulates in a
later revision carrying an erratum note; cite whichever version you consulted.

**[7]** The first author publishes under a single name, Hadiyawarman, which is
given here without initials. This is correct and should not be "fixed" by a
copy-editor.

**[26] and [27]** measure different quantities and the text keeps them apart.
[27] is run-to-run reproducibility of a co-sputtered composition spread, the
direct analogue of the deposition scatter our model applies, but for an oxide.
[26] is the systematic radial variation of Sc cation fraction across a nitride
wafer, which is a gradient rather than scatter. Do not merge them into a single
"uniformity" figure.
